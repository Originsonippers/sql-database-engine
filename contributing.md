Learn query parsing, the B-tree index, the buffer pool, the write-ahead log, MVCC, and the query planner — with runnable examples and diagrams. Built for backend engineers, database enthusiasts, and computer science students who want to understand what happens under `SELECT`. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.

Most SQL tutorials teach syntax, not the engine. **This guide explains the engine** — parser, planner, executor, B-tree index, buffer pool, WAL, and MVCC — with diagrams and runnable experiments. Every layer is explained with a real query.
